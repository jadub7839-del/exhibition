Facilities & amenities

Included with every stall

Every booked stall comes with the following, set up and running before gates open each day.

  • Electricity point with generator backup
  • Running water supply nearby
  • Gas cylinder station (BBQ aisle)
  • Shared cold storage access
  • One table and two chairs
  • Stall signage board with your name
  • Clean washroom blocks
  • Dedicated exhibitor parking
  • 24-hour security staff
  • Fire extinguisher at every counter
  • First aid station on ground
  • Public announcement system

Rules & guidelines

Exhibitor guidelines

Setup & timing

Stalls must be fully set up by 3:30 PM daily. Exhibitor entry opens at 2:00 PM each day.

Food safety

All food handlers must carry a valid health certificate. Punjab Food Authority inspections apply on all three days.

Stall boundaries

Displays, signage and seating must stay within your booked stall dimensions. Aisles must remain clear.

Power usage

Only pre-approved appliances may run on the stall connection. Overloading the point is not permitted.

Cancellation

Bookings are refundable up to 7 days before the event, less a 10% processing deduction.

Cleanliness

Exhibitors are responsible for clearing waste into the designated bins throughout the day.

Questions

Before you book

How do I book a stall?

Pick an available stall on the map, enter your details, choose how you'll pay, review everything, then confirm. You'll get a booking reference at the end. The whole thing takes about two minutes.

What payment methods can I use?

Bank transfer, JazzCash, EasyPaisa, credit or debit card, or cash at the exhibition office. Transfers and wallet payments need the transaction reference from your receipt so we can match the payment to your booking.

What's included with a stall?

An electricity point with generator backup, water access nearby, one table and two chairs, a signage board with your name, shared cold storage, a fire extinguisher, and exhibitor parking. BBQ-aisle stalls also get a gas cylinder station. The full list is in Facilities above.

Can I cancel, and do I get a refund?

Yes, up to 7 days before the exhibition — that's 5 December 2026. A 10% processing deduction applies. Cancellations after that date aren't refundable.

When can I set up my stall?

Exhibitor access opens at 2:00 PM each day and stalls must be ready by 3:30 PM, when gates open to the public at 4:00 PM. Come on the earlier side for day one — first-day setup always takes longer than people expect.

When will my booking be confirmed?

Reserving a stall holds it for 48 hours. Once we've verified your payment we send written confirmation within one working day. If payment doesn't arrive within 48 hours the stall goes back on the map.

Can I choose which stall I get?

Yes — you pick the exact stall on the floor plan, and it's held for you from the moment you select it. Prices vary by aisle because stall sizes and services differ; each category's price is shown above.
