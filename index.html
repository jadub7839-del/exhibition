The Exhibition

Three Days of Pure Desi Flavour

Zaiqa Mela brings together Pakistan's finest home chefs, street-food legends, and heritage restaurants under one roof (well, one open-air ground) — for a weekend built entirely around the art of desi cooking. From sizzling BBQ counters to century-old halwa recipes, every stall tells a story.

Rules & Guidelines

Exhibitor Guidelines

01. Setup & Timing

Stalls must be fully set up by 3:30 PM daily. Exhibitor entry begins at 2:00 PM each day.

02. Food Safety

All food handlers must carry a valid health certificate. Punjab Food Authority checks apply.

03. Stall Boundaries

Displays, signage, and seating must remain within your booked stall dimensions.

04. Power Usage

Only pre-approved appliances are allowed on the electricity connection. Overloading is prohibited.

05. Cancellations

Bookings are refundable up to 7 days before the event, minus a 10% processing fee.

06. Cleanliness

Exhibitors are responsible for waste disposal in designated bins throughout the day.
